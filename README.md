

# Hi, I'm Jennifer Agu

I am a biotechnologist interested in **science, sustainability and environmental issues**, particularly in an African context.

My background is in **Microbiology and Biotechnology**, and I have worked across research, sustainability and HSE. 

Recently, I have been writing about environmental and agricultural issues in Nigeria and learning more about how science, policy and sustainability connect.

I am especially interested in:

- Sustainable food and agricultural systems
- Environmental sustainability
- Biotechnology and biosafety
- Science communication
- Agri-tech and agribusiness

## Projects

# Cancer Metabolism and Physical Activity
My MSc computational biology project looking at the relationship between physical activity and cancer metabolism.

### Food Microbiology and Antibiotic Resistance
Research into *Shigella* and *Salmonella* isolated from ready-to-eat food, including antibiotic susceptibility and biofilm formation.

### GMOs and World Opinion
A review of GMOs in Nigeria, looking at public perception, regulation, food biotechnology and some of the arguments around health and environmental impacts.

## Publications

*   **[A Review of GMO Perception and Regulation in Nigeria](https://www.sustainabilitynewsafrica.com/articles/a-review-of-gmo-perception-and-regulation-in-nigeria)** — *Sustainability News Africa (2026).* Audited the operational disconnect between national biosafety policies and public acceptance of GMOs. Identified critical legislative escape clauses within the NBMA Act 2015, detailing the 20-year transition loop into deregulated environmental releases.
*   **[Fashion, Identity and Sustainability](https://blog.lboro.ac.uk/sustainability/2024/04/16/fashion-identity-and-sustainability/)** — *Loughborough University (2024).* Written as a Sustainability Ambassador, this publication critiqued  the environmental toll of linear textile consumption. It also suggested ways to mitigate global waste and support community sustainability.
*   **[FutureCasting Workshop by LU Arts & Studio Hyte](https://jenchioma.substack.com/p/futurecasting-workshop-by-lu-arts)** — *Loughborough University Arts × Studio Hyte, 2024.* Participated in a workshop exploring sustainable exhibition design, carbon impacts of digital design, audience-centred design and ethical approaches to innovation.
